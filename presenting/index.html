01  Lease Up Support

What if you're leasing up?

A leasing AI on its own doesn't know the three things every lease-up depends on

01

When homes will actually exist

Pre-leasing and move-in dates live in a project plan, not the PMS. Until units go live, there's nothing to sell.

✕"We don't have availability right now."
✓"Pre-leasing opens this winter. First move-ins Jan 12."
02

What to sell before units go live

Floor plans, price ranges and lease-up specials, before a single unit is live.

✕"Pricing isn't available yet."
✓"Two-beds start at $2,950, with 6 weeks free."
03

Who to hold for later

Prospects for homes that aren't released yet need an interest list, not a dead end.

✕Lead goes cold and is marked lost.
✓"You're on the Phase 3 list. First pick of floors."
